📡 Integration Tracker — now a standing weekly report
https://bstars00-rgb.github.io/Integration-Tracker/
Since late August the tracker has run every Saturday without anyone touching it — it pulls the Dev Schedule from SharePoint, rebuilds the board, and posts a different summary to each of the three channels. Eight consecutive runs now, and it caught every go-live along the way: Klook, Momo and Suzhou Yilv.
It is steady enough to depend on, so from here it is a regular weekly report rather than something produced on request.
What lands in this channel
Only the integrations where OMH engineering owes something — a DEV key, a certification pass, a live key, or launch monitoring. Partner-side work and sales-stage prospects stay out. A go-live is announced once and then drops out for good, so nothing repeats week after week.
Thank you for the five suggestions
Four are in:
  •  Dev Owner — OMH vs partner is read from the sheet now, not inferred from effort
  •  Target Go-Live — column is live and the board reports how far ahead or behind
  •  Hold/Drop — its own column, since Status is a formula and cannot be typed into
  •  Owner now and Next action — stage owner rather than the end-to-end PIC, and the next step derived from the nine-phase process
The fifth — marking stages N/A per partner type and recalculating — is still open. It needs the team to say which stages do not apply to Switching, CRS and 3rd Party. Those three record almost no stage dates today, so it cannot be inferred from the data.
The one thing still missing
Waiting On is empty on every row. The tracker can say a partner has not moved in 300 days, but not why — and the why is the part that would make this weekly genuinely useful to you rather than just accurate.
Where this should go next
The tracker reads a spreadsheet. That works, but the data is entered in one place and the work happens in another, which is why a column rename can quietly break a report and why the board is always a snapshot rather than the truth.
The better end state is this living inside Ellis — integration status as a first-class object in our own admin, updated where the work actually happens instead of re-keyed into Excel afterwards. With MCP, Claude could then read and write that directly: no weekly file sync, no matching on column names, no week-old snapshot. The weekly summary becomes a view over live data rather than a rebuild from a download.
Nothing to action now — putting it on the table for whenever the Ellis roadmap comes up.
